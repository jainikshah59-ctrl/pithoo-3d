HOW TO PLAY

  1. Break the stack. Drag back anywhere and release to throw the ball at the 7-stone tower. You get 3 throws.
  2. Rebuild it. Drag to run. Walk over a stone to pick it up, carry it to the glowing ring to stack it.
  3. Dodge the ball. Defenders pass and throw at you. Getting hit drops your stone and costs a life — you have 3.
  4. Your mates help but they are slower — the hero (you) stacks fastest.
  5. Win by stacking all 7 stones before time runs out. Then the next round gets tougher.
